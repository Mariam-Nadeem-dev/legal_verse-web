# legal_verse-web
Legal Verse  web - Pakistani law information webstite 
Legal Verse ⚖️
Legal Verse is a specialized web platform designed for lawyers, legal researchers, and law students in Pakistan. It simplifies legal research by offering an accessible, organized, and searchable repository for Pakistani case law citations and the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973.

🌟 Key Features
Constitution of Pakistan: Complete, chapter-wise, and article-wise browsing of the 1973 Constitution.

Case Law Search: Powerful search filters to locate case citations by keyword, title, year, court, or relevant statutes.

Smart Citation Finder: Fast lookup system optimized for Pakistani legal citation formats (e.g., PLD, SCMR, CLC, PCrLJ, YLR).

Bookmark & Save: Allows legal practitioners to save frequently cited articles and case precedents for quick reference.

Responsive Interface: Modern, mobile-friendly UI tailored for fast access on smartphones, tablets, or desktop devices during court proceedings.
